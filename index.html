About Us

Masa Neubotics Technology was founded by Siti Nursyuhada Mahsahirun on August 8, 2022, in Pekan, Pahang, Malaysia, while she was pursuing her PhD. Her research focused on the implementation of Artificial Neural Networks in induction motor drive systems.

Siti earned her Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering from UMP in 2010, formerly known as UTM-Pahang and later KUKTEM. However, BEM did not recognise her undergraduate degree, and she was therefore unable to register as a graduate electrical engineer in Malaysia. She continued her studies and earned a Master's Degree in Mechatronic Engineering by Research in power electronics and induction motor drives from the same university in 2016.

Due to limited resources, she was unable to complete her PhD. In 2023, she returned to her home state of Sabah as a PhD dropout and continued building the business from scratch.

In 2024, as the business faced difficulties sustaining itself due to multi-level politics and bureaucratic hurdles, she established Ninja Market, an online retail and services platform. In 2026, she founded De Geeks and Jalur Lintang to explore photolithography and software development. Masaneubotics continously growing into diverse business branches that includes Sukaria Entertainment, Motor Geliga, Dapur Makcik and Kebun Bapak.

Before Masa Neubotics, Siti had already been providing voluntary assistance to the community in Tanjung Selangor, Pekan. As children from surrounding villages began coming to her home to learn Quran, the community gradually grew and became informally known as Sahabat IQRA.

In 2025, Siti applied to register IQRA Community with the Registrar of Societies (ROS), but the application was rejected. Today, IQRA Community continues as the official CSR brand of Masa Neubotics Technology, committed to building communities that uphold the values of innovation, quintessential, resilience, and advancement.
