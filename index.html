About Us

Masa Neubotics Technology was founded by Siti Nursyuhada Mahsahirun on August 8, 2022, in Pekan, Pahang, Malaysia, while she was pursuing her PhD. Her research focused on the implementation of Artificial Neural Networks in induction motor drive systems.

Siti earned her Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering from UMP in 2010, formerly known as UTM-Pahang and later KUKTEM. She continued her studies and earned a Master’s Degree in Mechatronic Engineering by Research from the same university in 2016.

Due to limited resources, she was unable to complete her PhD. In 2023, she returned to her home state of Sabah as a PhD dropout and continued building the business from scratch.

In 2024, as the business faced difficulties sustaining itself, she established Ninja Market, an online retail and services platform. In 2026, she founded De Geeks and Jalur Lintang to explore photolithography and software development.

Before Masa Neubotics, Siti had already been providing voluntary assistance to the community in Tanjung Selangor, Pekan. As children from surrounding villages began coming to her home to learn Quran, the community gradually grew and became informally known as Sahabat IQRA.

In 2025, Siti applied to register IQRA Community with the Registrar of Societies (ROS), but the application was rejected. Today, IQRA Community continues as the official CSR brand of Masa Neubotics Technology, with a commitment to building communities that uphold the values of intelligence, quintessential, resilience, and advancement.
